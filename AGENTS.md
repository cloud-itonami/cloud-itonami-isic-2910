# AGENTS.md

## CI/CD: no JVM / nbb (JUN-290, removed in JUN-717)

Workspace rule (owner, 2026-10-22, JUN-290): JVM and nbb are not used, so CI/CD
must not start setup-java, setup-clojure, clojure, nbb, babashka or GraalVM.
Such workflows are deleted, not disabled or kept as a fallback. The canonical
rule is the "CI/CD（CDCI）に JVM と nbb を置かない" section of the workspace
`AGENTS.md` (canonical source: `com-junkawasaki/root/AGENTS.md`, "CDCI 停止中").

- **Removed:**
  - `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: job `lint` (setup-java 21 + setup-clojure,
    `clojure -M:lint`) and job `test` (sibling checkouts of kotoba-lang/
    langgraph, langchain, robotics, html, css, then `clojure -M:dev:test`).
  - `.github/workflows/regenerate.yml` (`regenerate-console`): nightly
    schedule that ran `clojure -M:dev:render-html`, sanity-checked
    `docs/samples/operator-console.html` and committed it when changed. Its
    non-JVM steps only checked and committed the JVM step's output, so
    nothing of value remained and the whole file was removed.
- **Kept:** `.github/workflows/pages.yml` (static GitHub Pages deploy of
  `docs/`; no JVM, nbb or babashka).
- **Native gate (Amu selfhost):** none. This repository has no Amu selfhost
  native gate with a negative control; that is recorded as blocked.
- **No longer covered in CI:**
  - clj-kondo lint of `src`, `test`, `test-cross-repo` (`:lint`, errors fail);
  - the `test/` suite (governor, store parity, export, phases, facts) via
    cognitect test-runner (`:dev:test`);
  - nightly regeneration of `docs/samples/operator-console.html` through the
    real actor (`automotive.render-html`) and its sanity checks. The console
    is no longer refreshed automatically; it stays at its last committed
    rendering until regenerated by other means.
